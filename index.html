What the exam looks like

The professor's Fall 2025 Midterm 2 and his practice Midterm 2 each had 5 questions worth 10 points each, with no calculators, and the instructions said to write complete, clear, legible solutions. He has said this year's exam will have different questions and may stress different topics, so every section in the list above is fair game. Both of his exams are solved in full in the Exams unit.

Between them, those two exams asked for: a curve sketch with extrema and inflection points labeled, intervals of increase and decrease, a chain rule derivative, two implicit differentiation problems, a differentials error estimate, an elasticity problem with an explanation, a related rates problem, and two optimization word problems with a justification that the answer is the minimum.

How to spend 8 hours
  1. Hour 1. Units 1 to 3 (derivative rules, chain rule, exponentials and logs). Read each lesson, then do Levels 1 and 2. These rules are used in every later problem, so they have to be automatic.
  2. Hour 2. Unit 4 (trig derivatives and L'Hospital's rule) and Unit 5 (increasing, decreasing, relative extrema).
  3. Hour 3. Unit 6 (concavity and the second derivative test) and Unit 7 (curve sketching). Do every Level 4 sketch on paper.
  4. Hour 4. Unit 8 (absolute extrema) and Unit 9 (optimization). Write the justification sentence every time.
  5. Hour 5. Unit 10 (elasticity and order quantity) and Unit 11 (implicit differentiation).
  6. Hour 6. Unit 12 (related rates) and Unit 13 (differentials).
  7. Hour 7. The professor's two exams, timed, then check against the full solutions. Go back to Levels 3 and 4 of any unit where you lost points.
  8. Hour 8. One mock midterm under exam conditions (60 minutes, no notes, no calculator), then the memorize page.

If you only have a short session, do Level 4 of Units 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13. Those six problem types made up almost all of both of his exams.
